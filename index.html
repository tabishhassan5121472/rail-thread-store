Made to last.

We make fewer things, and we make them heavier. Every piece on the rail is knitted from long-staple cotton, garment-dyed in small batches and sewn with double-needle seams built to outlive the trend cycle.

  1. 01

    Yarn

    Long-staple cotton, ring-spun so the surface stays smooth after a hundred washes.

  2. 02

    Loopback fleece knitted slowly on circular machines — dense, springy, unapologetically heavy.

  3. 03

    Dye

    Garment-dyed after sewing, so every seam softens and fades in step with the body.

  4. 04
